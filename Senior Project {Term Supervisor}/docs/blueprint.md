# **App Name**: Term Supervisor

## Core Features:

- Schedule Visualization: Visually represent the student's term schedule, displaying courses, deadlines, and key milestones in a calendar format.
- Schedule Editing: Allow students to add, modify, and remove courses and deadlines in the term schedule.
- Deadline Reminders: Send notifications and reminders for upcoming deadlines.
- AI Term Optimizer: AI tool recommends an optimal order of courses for the student, maximizing the potential learning outcomes.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A calm blue (#6699CC), reminiscent of clear skies and focus.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F0F0) for a clean, distraction-free environment.
- Accent color: A muted teal (#468499) to highlight important deadlines and milestones.
- Clean, sans-serif fonts for optimal readability on schedules.
- Use of universally understandable icons to represent course categories and deadlines.
- Clean and spacious design to enhance the visualization of schedules and avoid clutter.
